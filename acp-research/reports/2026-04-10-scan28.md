### 2.2 #1717 governance metadata — 生产级「skill-scoped trust scores」成为共识

**新评论摘要:**
- `@JKHeadley` (MoltBridge): 从生产经验出发，提出 **skill-scoped trust scores** 比 aggregate scores 更有价值
  > "An agent might be excellent at code review (trust: 0.95) but unreliable at real-time data feeds (trust: 0.42)"
- `@MoltyCel` (MoltTrust): 同意，并给出 REST 接口草案 `GET /skill/trust-score/{did}?capability=code_review`
- `@kevinkaylie` (AgentNexus): Enclave 的 `role → permissions → skill` 映射提供可验证能力清单

**与 ACP 的 gap:**
- ACP RFC-003 目前 `governance_metadata.trust_score` 是单一浮点数（0–1）
- 社区正在收敛到 **per-skill trust score** 结构
- **建议 v2.95 候选:** `trust_score` 改为 `dict[skill_id → float]` 或 `skill_scores: [{skill_id, score, updated_at}]`
